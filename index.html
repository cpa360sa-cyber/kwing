Built for the businesses on your street

Most kasi customers already order by WhatsApp: a voice note here, a "you open?" there. KWING turns those chats into proper orders, so nothing gets lost while you're busy behind the counter.

How it works

From "Eita" to "it's ready" in four taps.

The bot does the typing. You do the cooking. Counter orders from the till land on the same board.

  1. Customer WhatsApps you

    In English, isiZulu, Sesotho or slang. The bot only offers what's on your menu today.

    "2 Russian kotas and a Coke"

  2. The bot confirms the order

    It reads back every item and the total in rand, and only places the order after the customer says yes.

    "Total R98. Shall I place it?"

  3. It pops up in the kitchen

    The ticket appears on your kitchen board straight away, with a green WhatsApp badge and the delivery address if there is one.

    #1043 · New

  4. Tap "Ready"

    The customer gets a WhatsApp message to come and fetch it, or that it's on the way.

    "Sho Sipho! Your order is ready"

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need a new phone number?

The bot needs a number that isn't on the normal WhatsApp app. Many shops get a cheap new SIM for orders, or move their existing business number over. We'll help you choose.

Does the bot understand slang and isiZulu?

Yes. It replies in the language the customer uses, and in your shop's voice. It never makes up items or prices: it only sells what's on your menu and not marked sold out.

What if a customer changes their mind?

The bot always reads back the order and total and waits for a clear "yes" before placing it. Changes after that go to a staff member, the same as a phone call.

Who owns the devices?

We buy the devices and lend them to you for the 24-month contract. Your monthly fee pays them off, and after 24 months they're yours and the fee drops. If a device breaks from normal use, we fix or swap it. Damage, loss and theft are your responsibility, so add the devices to your business insurance.

Can I cancel before 24 months?

Yes. Either return the devices in working order and pay what's still owed on them, or keep the devices and settle that amount. Your quote shows exactly what you'd owe each month.

Can it print receipts?

Yes. Connect a small thermal printer (58 or 80 mm, Bluetooth or USB) to print customer slips and kitchen tickets after every sale, and open a cash drawer on cash sales. VAT-registered shops can print tax invoices.

Can it take card payments?

Yes. With a Yoco account, customers pay by card or instant EFT through a QR code at the till or a link on WhatsApp, and the order is marked paid by itself. You can also keep using your usual card machine (Yoco, iKhokha or SnapScan) and tap "Card" on the till.

I have more than one shop. Does it work?

Yes. Head office sets one menu, and customers use one WhatsApp number. The bot asks which branch they're collecting from, and each branch gets its own kitchen board, stock and prices. Owners see sales for every branch.

Who owns my customer data?

You do. Your orders and customers' numbers are kept in your own database, not shared with other shops. We help you follow POPIA.

What happens when the internet goes down?

KWING needs internet to save orders and receive WhatsApp messages. When the connection comes back, the board catches up by itself.
